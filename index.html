About Us

A vertically integrated platform built for every stage of real estate

Vega unifies investment, development, construction, management, and brokerage under one roof — creating value at every point in the real estate lifecycle.

Our Mission

To deliver institutional-quality real estate outcomes through vertically integrated operations — from capital deployment and entitlement through construction, management, and disposition.

Our History

Built from the ground up as an owner-operator, Vega has scaled into eight specialized business units spanning private equity, development, construction, brokerage, and asset management.

Our Values

Discipline, transparency, and alignment of interest drive every decision. We invest alongside our partners and operate with the rigor of institutional capital and the agility of an entrepreneurial team.
