Our Approach

Vega Assisted Living Management was built by people who understand what owners need from an operator. Measurable outcomes, honest reporting, and a team that treats the building like the investment it is. The path to strong occupancy and retention runs through people, not spreadsheets.

We staff to efficiency. The right people in the right roles at the right ratios, not an arbitrary headcount. We assign caregivers to the same residents so genuine relationships can form. We report monthly with full transparency, because owners who see the real numbers make better decisions, and operators who share them hold themselves to a higher standard.

About

A Real Estate Operator That Learned to Run Care.

Vega ALM started because a real estate firm saw what happens when a third-party operator does not share the same priorities as the building owner. What began as an internal solution became a standalone management company grounded in operational discipline and a staffing-first philosophy.

Our History
2012
Founded

Vega begins as a real estate investment firm focused on senior housing, learning the industry from the ownership side.

2016
First Operation

Takes over management of a first community after a third-party operator underperforms. The experience shapes the operating model.

2019
Formalized

ALM established as a standalone operating company within the Vega platform, with its own leadership and clinical team.

2024
Scale

Portfolio grows across the Mountain West, earning the trust of building owners through consistent, transparent operations.

Vega Platform

Built by Vega. Run Independently.

Vega ALM operates as an independent management company within the Vega platform. Our leadership team makes operating decisions without layers of approval, which means faster responses to the realities on the ground in each community.

The Vega platform provides shared infrastructure: legal, accounting, human resources, and capital markets expertise. That support lets our operators focus on what they do best, which is running communities and delivering results for owners.

Ownership alignment is built into the structure. Vega invests in many of the communities we manage, which means our interests as operator and owner move in the same direction.

Careers

The Staffing Plan Is the Care Plan.

We believe the quality of care in a community is determined by the people who show up every day. That is why we invest in staffing ratios, training, and a culture where caregivers feel valued, stay longer, and grow into new roles.

Why Work Here

Published Ratios

We staff above state minimums and hold ourselves to published caregiver-to-resident ratios. You will never be asked to work short-staffed.

Promote From Within

Every community leader started on the floor. We fund training and promote before we hire outside, because the best leaders already understand the work.

Tuition and Licensure

CNA certification, med-tech training, and LPN tuition assistance are available from day one. We want you to build a career here, and we will invest in helping you get there.

Consistent Assignment

Same Residents, Same Caregivers.

Our consistent assignment model means caregivers work with the same group of residents every shift. That continuity builds genuine trust, helps you notice changes in condition earlier, and makes the work more meaningful.

Rotating assignments might look efficient on a schedule, but they come at the cost of the relationships that define good care. We choose consistency over convenience, and we see the difference in retention for both residents and staff.
