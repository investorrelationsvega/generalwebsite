Our Approach

Vega ALM was born out of a real estate investment firm. That background gives us a bias toward measurable outcomes: occupancy, retention, NOI. But we learned early that the path to those numbers runs through people, not spreadsheets. Operational discipline only works when it is built on a culture that retains staff and earns the trust of residents and their families.

We staff above state minimums and publish our ratios. We assign caregivers to the same residents so relationships can form. We report monthly with full transparency, because owners who see the real numbers make better decisions, and operators who share them hold themselves to a higher standard.

We use a standardized chart of accounts across all communities so comparisons are meaningful and variance analysis is straightforward.

Quarterly, we add a capital expenditure review and a staffing audit. Annual budgets are built collaboratively with each ownership group.

Vega Platform

Built by Vega. Run Independently.

Vega ALM operates as an independent management company within the Vega platform. Our leadership team makes operating decisions without layers of approval, which means faster responses to the realities on the ground in each community.

The Vega platform provides shared infrastructure: legal, accounting, human resources, and capital markets expertise. That lets our operators focus on what they do best, which is running buildings and taking care of residents.

Ownership alignment is built into the structure. Vega invests in many of the communities we manage, which means our interests as operator and owner are never in conflict.

Consistent Assignment

Same Residents, Same Caregivers.

Our consistent assignment model means caregivers work with the same group of residents every shift. That continuity builds trust, catches changes in condition earlier, and makes the work more meaningful.

Rotating assignments might look efficient on a schedule, but they erode the relationships that define good care. We choose consistency over convenience, and we see it in our retention numbers for both residents and staff.
