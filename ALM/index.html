Our Approach

Vega ALM grew out of a real estate investment firm that saw firsthand what happens when operations fall short. That experience gave us a deep respect for measurable outcomes, but it also taught us that the path to strong occupancy and retention runs through people, not spreadsheets.

We staff above state minimums and publish our ratios. We assign caregivers to the same residents so genuine relationships can form. We report monthly with full transparency, because owners who see the real numbers make better decisions, and operators who share them hold themselves to a higher standard. When the people who live and work in our communities feel cared for, everything else follows.

Reporting

Monthly Transparency, No Exceptions

Every owner receives a complete financial package by the 15th of the following month. We use a standardized chart of accounts across all communities so comparisons are meaningful and variance analysis is straightforward.

Quarterly, we add a capital expenditure review and a staffing audit. Annual budgets are built collaboratively with each ownership group, because the best plans come from working together.

Monthly Financial Package

A community-level profit and loss statement, census and occupancy dashboard, and a rolling 12-month trend report. Delivered by the 15th of each month, every month.

Quarterly Reviews

Capital expenditure tracking, staffing audits, and a review of operating performance against the annual budget, with variance explanations for every line that moves.

Annual Budget Collaboration

We build each year's budget alongside ownership, aligning on staffing plans, capital priorities, and occupancy targets before the year begins.

On-Demand Access

Owners have access to real-time census data and financial dashboards at any time. You should never have to wait for a scheduled report to know how your community is performing.

All reporting is standardized across communities for easy comparison and portfolio-level analysis.

About

Born From Experience. Built Around People.

Vega ALM started because a real estate firm saw what happens when the people running a community do not share the same priorities as the people living in one. What began as an internal solution became a standalone management company built around a simple idea: when you take care of the people, the building takes care of itself.

Our History
2012
Founded

Vega begins as a real estate investment firm focused on senior housing, learning the industry from the ownership side.

2016
First Community

Vega takes over management of its first assisted living community after seeing firsthand that residents deserved better day-to-day care.

2019
Formalized

Assisted Living Management is established as a standalone operating company within the Vega platform, with its own leadership and clinical team.

2024
Growing

The team grows to serve communities across multiple states, earning the trust of owners and families through consistent, compassionate operations.

Vega Platform

Built by Vega. Run Independently.

Vega ALM operates as an independent management company within the Vega platform. Our leadership team makes operating decisions without layers of approval, which means faster responses to the realities on the ground in each community.

The Vega platform provides shared infrastructure: legal, accounting, human resources, and capital markets expertise. That support lets our operators focus on what they do best, which is running communities and taking care of residents.

Ownership alignment is built into the structure. Vega invests in many of the communities we manage, which means our interests as operator and owner move in the same direction.

Careers

The Staffing Plan Is the Care Plan.

We believe the quality of care in a community is determined by the people who show up every day. That is why we invest in staffing ratios, training, and a culture where caregivers feel valued, stay longer, and grow into new roles.

Why Work Here

Published Ratios

We staff above state minimums and publish our caregiver-to-resident ratios publicly. You will never be asked to work short-staffed, because we know that is not fair to you or the people you care for.

Promote From Within

Every community leader started on the floor. We fund training and promote before we hire outside, because the best leaders are the ones who already understand the work.

Tuition and Licensure

CNA certification, med-tech training, and LPN tuition assistance are available from day one. We want you to build a career here, and we will invest in helping you get there.

Community Photo
Consistent Assignment

Same Residents, Same Caregivers.

Our consistent assignment model means caregivers work with the same group of residents every shift. That continuity builds genuine trust, helps you notice changes in a resident's condition earlier, and makes the work more meaningful.

Rotating assignments might look efficient on a schedule, but they come at the cost of the relationships that define good care. We choose consistency over convenience, and we see the difference in our retention numbers for both residents and staff.
