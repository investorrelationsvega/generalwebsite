Common Questions

What Owners Want to Know

What types of properties does Vega manage?

We manage multifamily, commercial, and mixed-use properties. Whether you own a single building or multiple assets, our services scale to fit the scope and complexity of your property.

What is included in your management agreement?

Our standard agreement covers leasing and marketing, maintenance and vendor coordination, monthly financial reporting, tenant relations, compliance and risk management, and asset management strategy. Everything you need to operate the property and protect its long-term value is included.

What does asset management include?

Our asset management services include market positioning analysis, capital improvement evaluation, hold-sell strategy, and long-term return planning. We help owners see the full picture so every operating decision supports asset value and return targets.

How much do you charge for property management?

Our fees are competitive and negotiable based on the size and complexity of your property. We work with each owner to structure a fee arrangement that aligns with their goals. Contact us at (801) 921-5746 for a custom quote.

How does your financial reporting work?

We deliver transparent, owner-ready financial statements every month. Each report includes income, expenses, and variance analysis in a format you can act on. Reports are delivered on a fixed schedule so you always know when to expect them.
